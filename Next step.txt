Things I would still change
There aren't many. I would now stop redesigning sections and move into refinement.
1. Hero responsiveness — this is the biggest thing we need to test. The desktop composition is excellent, but we need to see what happens around 1440, 1024, 768, 430, 390 and 360 px.
2. 3D performance — lazy-load the model, optimize the GLB/textures, cap DPR, provide sensible mobile behavior and make sure it doesn't destroy Lighthouse performance.
3. Animations — right now the screenshot looks polished, but screenshots can't communicate interaction. This should be our next visual-development stage. Add subtle scroll reveal animations rather than making everything fly around.
4. Project cards — eventually use actual project screenshots/previews. That will make this section much stronger than adding decorative 3D models.
5. Contact form — make it actually send messages, with loading/success/error states and validation.
6. CV download — verify that the PDF really downloads in production.
7. Accessibility/SEO — semantic structure, keyboard interaction, reduced-motion handling, meta information, Open Graph image, favicon, descriptive alt text, etc.
8. Deployment and final testing.
